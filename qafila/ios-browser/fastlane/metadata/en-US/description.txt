Qafila (قافلة) means "caravan" in Arabic.

Qafila is a fast, private and fully customizable browser for iPhone.

PUT EVERY BUTTON WHERE YOU WANT IT
• Choose the buttons in the toolbar and in the menu, their order and size
• Add separators to organize the menu into sections
• Toolbar gallery with ready-made layouts: Complete, Minimal, Gestures, Reading
• Toolbar at the bottom or top, Classic or Glass style

BEAUTIFUL, TIDY TABS
• Tabs grouped by site, as a list or a grid of thumbnails
• Sort by date, reading time or title
• Private tabs, never saved in history

BROWSE WITHOUT BEING TRACKED
• Built-in ad and tracker blocker
• Cookie banner hiding
• JavaScript on or off, per site
• Automatic HTTPS upgrade
• No data collection, no account, no third-party service

READ COMFORTABLY
• Dark mode for every website, globally or per site
• Reader view that extracts the article
• Estimated reading time in the address bar and the tab list
• Bars hide while scrolling for full-screen reading
• Desktop site, translation, find in page

MAKE IT YOURS
• Interface color that adapts to the website, 8 accent colors
• Search engine of your choice: DuckDuckGo, Qwant, Kagi, Brave, SearXNG, Google, Bing or your own Qafila instance
• Configurable new tab page

Qafila uses WebKit, the engine behind Safari: websites render exactly as you expect. Every feature is free.
